Objective: To send vaccine reminders and recalls to a client or caregiver notifying that the client is due, due on a future date, or past due for a vaccine (see Fig. 9). 
This is typically done electronically and automatically. 
For clients already electronically registered, the same process may also be used to notify them of a new vaccine that is available for which they are eligible. This 
could include relevant disease specific vaccines (such as those for COVID-19 or seasonal influenza). This would be more of a notification than a reminder.
